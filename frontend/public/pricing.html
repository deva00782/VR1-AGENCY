Website Maintenance & Support

Keep your website secure, updated and running smoothly with ongoing monthly support.

Maintenance plans cover ongoing website support and minor updates. New pages, major redesigns, new modules, booking systems, payment integrations and other major features are quoted separately.

What Does Website Maintenance Include?

Content Updates

Text changes, image replacements, adding new team members, or updating service descriptions.

Security

Monitoring for vulnerabilities, SSL renewals, and protecting against malicious attacks.

Backups

Regular database and file backups to ensure your website can be restored in case of emergency.

Performance

Monitoring load times and fixing any speed issues that arise as browsers update.

Technical Support

Troubleshooting bugs, fixing broken links, and resolving email/hosting issues.

Minor Improvements

Small layout tweaks, CSS adjustments, or adding simple promotional banners.

What's Not Included?

  • Major structural redesigns (e.g. "change the entire homepage layout")
  • Creating entirely new pages with unique designs
  • Adding new complex features (e.g. adding a booking system, e-commerce store, or custom user portal)
  • Writing new copy or creating original graphics/videos from scratch
  • Extensive SEO overhauls (we offer separate SEO retainers for this)

If you need these services, we will provide a separate, transparent quote before beginning any work.
